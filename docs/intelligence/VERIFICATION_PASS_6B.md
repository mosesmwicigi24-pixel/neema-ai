## Verdict

**Not safe to deploy as it stands.** The detector itself is sound in the dangerous direction — across 132 adversarial messages with an ask, an address, a name, a date, a number, a negation or a complaint after the pleasantry, none is silenced, and the blind/tuning numbers re-derive exactly — but the production wiring undoes the change for the forms the slice adds: a "Received" / "I see" / "Thank you for your time" / "Nimeelewa" / "Mungu awabariki" on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram is silenced at the gate, left as the thread's latest unanswered inbound with no silence marker, and re-answered by the missed-reply sweeper within a few minutes by a model turn that is not even told it is a closer (or by the stock "One moment — let me check on that for you." line), with a human escalation on Meta after 23 h; this is the ping-pong the register says is over, measured by a runner that never runs the sweeper. Separately, the gate now silences a bare "ok"/"sawa" that answers Neema's own "Shall I start your order?", a case the benchmark contains zero instances of. Both are a few lines to fix (mark silenced on every gate `True`; teach `_answerable_turn` the same decision; let an affirmation after our own question through) and should be fixed and pinned with tests before the next real-model run, which would otherwise report silence the customers will not get.
